AI Marketing Agent

Client in Saudi Arabia, live in production

Sharia Base

Upload a financial contract, get back a compliant one: checked against Islamic jurisprudence and national law.

The problem

Checking a financial contract for Sharia compliance means weighing each clause against the four schools of thought, contemporary fiqh of financial transactions and the rulings of major Islamic bodies, then checking it again against national law.

Contracts run from one page to more than ten, and the review takes specialist knowledge. The users range from banks and companies to individuals contracting with each other.

What I built

An Agentic RAG system over a large knowledge base: references from the four schools, contemporary fiqh works on finance, publications of Islamic organizations, and works of senior scholars.

  • Validate: confirms the upload is a contract, and a financial one.
  • Extract: reads PDFs or images and splits the contract into clauses.
  • Classify: flags clauses with potential compliance issues.
  • Analyze: agents pinpoint each issue and the basis for the ruling.

What changed

  • Rewriteflagged clauses are corrected and a revised contract is produced
  • Basisevery flagged clause comes with the reasoning behind the ruling
  • 10+ ppcontracts from one page to more than ten, in one pass

My part: co-designed the system and its features, and built the data pipeline into the vector database, the validation and classification layers, and several of the analysis and correction agents.

Agentic RAG, LangGraph, LangChain, Qdrant, Gemini, OCR

Two factories: a global tea brand and a tyre manufacturer

Vision Quality Control

Real-time camera inspection that catches defects the moment they happen, not at the end of the line.

The problem

On a tea packaging line, a person stood at the conveyor checking every box by eye: dates, batch codes, open or crushed boxes.

At a tyre factory, punctured or folded rubber only showed up in the final stages, and by then the tyres had to be scrapped.

What I built

  • Tea line: checks that production date, expiry date and batch code are printed correctly and in the right place, and that the box isn't open or damaged. Defective boxes are pushed off the belt automatically.
  • Tyre line: detects punctured or folded rubber at an early, critical stage of production.

What changed

  • Linemanual inspection replaced, production faster, human error removed
  • Earlytyre defects caught before scrap losses that cost thousands of pounds a day
