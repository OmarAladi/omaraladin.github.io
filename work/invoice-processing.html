The problem

Every month, someone in each pharmacy had to go through more than 1,500 supplier invoices: reading each one, calculating it and entering it by hand.

What I built

A system that reads pharmaceutical invoices with high-accuracy OCR and turns them into structured data.

  • Extract. Pulls item IDs, quantities, tax and totals from each invoice.
  • Calculate. Works out the figures automatically.
  • Record. Enters everything without anyone retyping it.
