The problem

Checking a financial contract for Sharia compliance means weighing every clause against the four schools of thought, contemporary fiqh of financial transactions and the rulings of major Islamic bodies, and then checking it again against the country's law.

Contracts range from a single page to well over ten, and the review takes specialist knowledge. The people who need it range from banks and large companies to small businesses and individuals making agreements with each other.

How it works

  1. Validate. Confirms the upload is actually a contract, and that it deals with financial transactions.
  2. Extract. Reads the contract from a PDF or an image and identifies each clause.
  3. Classify. Flags the clauses that may have compliance problems.
  4. Analyze. Agents pinpoint where each problem is and the basis for the ruling.
  5. Rewrite. The problematic clauses are corrected and the contract is written out again.

The knowledge base

A large, curated knowledge base that the agents retrieve from:

  • References from the four schools of thought
  • Contemporary fiqh works on financial transactions
  • Publications of Islamic organizations and fiqh councils
  • Works of senior scholars
