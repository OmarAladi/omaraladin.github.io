AI Marketing Agent

An AI system that handles the repetitive side of digital marketing across Local SEO, SEO, social media and paid ads. A human approves anything before it goes public.

Client
US healthcare group, 30+ branches
My role
End to end, from discovery to deployment
Scope
4 modules, 9 agents and tools
Status
Built and deployed

The problem

Before writing any code, I shadowed the marketing team for a while to see exactly where their hours were going, and picked the problems worth automating from what I saw.

Four stood out. Business listings across 30+ branches could change without anyone noticing until it caused trouble. Reviews came in faster than the team could answer them, so some were answered late and some not at all. SEO problems were discovered long after they started. And ad budget kept running on campaigns that weren't paying off.

What I built

Local SEO

  • Change alerts. The team is notified as soon as any information on a branch's Google Business Profile changes, instead of finding out weeks later.
  • Review agent. Every new review is analyzed and classified, and a reply is drafted in the company's tone that takes the reviewer's situation into account. A team member approves or edits it before it's posted.
  • Branch dashboard. Google Business Profile and BrightLocal analytics combined into one dashboard per branch, so decisions take one look.

SEO

  • Meta tags agent. Runs every week on Search Console data, finds pages whose meta titles and descriptions are hurting them, and rewrites them. The team no longer has to guess whether what they wrote is working.
  • Content agent. Takes the missing-keywords report from competitor analysis in SEMrush, separates brand-relevant keywords from the rest, groups related ones into clusters, and drafts blog articles for review.
  • Page optimization agent. Takes the weak-keywords report, finds which pages are losing to competitors and on which keywords, and proposes how to strengthen each page's content.

Social media

  • Comments agent. Replies to Facebook and Instagram comments in the brand's tone. Negative comments, and comments where someone shared personal information they shouldn't have, are hidden until the team reviews them.
  • Messages agent. Replies to Facebook and Instagram messages and ignores spam links. The team follows every conversation from one page.

Paid ads

  • Campaign analyst. Reads campaign and ad results from Meta and Google Ads, summarizes them, and recommends which campaigns or ads to pause or scale, measured against benchmarks the team sets.

What changed

25%+of ad spend saved by pausing campaigns with high cost per lead
Weeklynew ready-to-review articles, up from aiming for 4 a month
30+branch listings watched, with changes caught right away
Everyreview gets a drafted reply, so none are missed

Decisions on ads got faster, SEO problems are caught early instead of months later, and the team spends its time reviewing and approving instead of searching and typing.
