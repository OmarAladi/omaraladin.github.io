Vision Quality Control

Real-time camera inspection that catches defects the moment they happen, not at the end of the line.

My role
Built the detection systems
Setting
Live production lines
Field
Computer vision

The problem

On a tea packaging line, a person had to stand at the conveyor and watch every box, pulling out the faulty ones by hand. It slowed the line and left room for human error.

At a tyre factory, punctured or folded rubber at one stage of production was only discovered in the final stages. By then the tyres had to be scrapped, with losses running into thousands of pounds a day.

What I built

Tea packaging line

Cameras on the line check every box as it passes:

  • Production date, expiry date and batch code are printed correctly and in the right place
  • The box isn't open
  • The box isn't crushed or deformed

Any box that fails is pushed off the belt automatically.

Tyre production line

Detects punctured or folded rubber at an early, critical stage of production, before the defect travels down the line.

What changed

Automaticinspection on the tea line, replacing the person who checked boxes by hand
Earlytyre defects caught before they become scrap
Fasterproduction on both lines, and faster to reach daily targets
